Developed a protoype for elders to build a healthy living habit with Figma and vibe coding. 

This app suppports: 

- Caring voice checkin
- Contactless sleeping monitoring
- Photo-based food recognition
- Customized pill reminder
- Virtual pet
- Bottle raise arm exercise (requires camera access)

[Try the app!](https://thomas-yip.github.io/EMIA2020-Elder-Man/#/)
